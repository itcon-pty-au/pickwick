Pickwick

A kid-safe, whitelist-only video player for Android phones, tablets and Google TV — parents choose exactly which YouTube channels exist; nothing else is reachable.

No adsNo ShortsNo comments No recommendationsNo accountsNo cloud
Download

What the kid sees

  • "Who's watching?" — each kid gets their own profile (color + avatar), with an optional secret remote-button code so siblings can't borrow each other's
  • A grid of parent-approved channels — and nothing else
  • 🎲 Surprise me and ❤️ My list — per kid
  • Keep watching across the family's devices
  • 📥 Offline downloads — kid asks, parent approves; works with no Wi-Fi
  • Fullscreen player with no ads and nothing to escape into — subtitles on demand
  • NEW badges when an allowed channel has fresh uploads
  • Gentle time warnings, friendly break & bedtime screens
  • Fully D-pad navigable on TV

What parents control

  • Kids — one profile per child; one shared channel list with per-kid switches, per-kid screen time, and devices dedicated to a kid or shared
  • Channels & playlists — search, add, or import & share whitelists
  • Discover with AI — describe what you want, get verified channel suggestions
  • AI content screening (optional) — your house rules, enforced on new videos with a verdict per kid, by age; bring your own key or local model
  • Screen time — sessions, breaks, bedtime, per-kid extra-time grants, pause-for-today
  • Time price tags — educational cheap, junk food expensive
  • Live stats per device, with a remote pause button
  • All from the parent's phone, fingerprint- or PIN-gated
  • Everything stays on your devices — there is no server

Free forever — but not maintenance-free

YouTube changes its internals every few weeks, and each time it does, playback breaks for every family until the app is updated and re-shipped. Donations fund that ongoing work.
