# v0.14.3 - Bigger channel cards in search

- **Channels and podcasts are easier to spot in search.** Names that match your search now appear as large cards with their logo, in a row you can scroll sideways, instead of small text chips. They're the same tiles as the home screen, so the **NEW** and screen-time badges come along too.
- **The cards scroll with the results.** The row moves out of the way as you scroll down through the videos.
- **On the TV, the remote starts on the first card** when a search matches a channel or podcast.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
