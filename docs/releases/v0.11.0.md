# v0.11.0 - Fire TV and quality picker

- **Fire TV support.** Pickwick now installs and runs on Fire OS 6 and newer (Android 7.1+), including the 1st-gen Fire TV Cube. Install it the same way as on Google TV, through Downloader.
- **Quality picker in the player.** On the TV remote, press ▼ and move to **HD**; on phones, tap the quality pill at the top right. Choose Auto or any height the video offers. The picture switches in place and your choice is remembered.
- **Videos made for kids often play at 360p.** In testing, YouTube offered only a single 360p stream for them, so that is the only choice the picker shows. Other videos offer the full range.
- **Fixes for small-memory boxes.** Channel data is no longer held in memory for every channel at once, which ran Fire TV boxes out of memory and sent the app back to the home screen after a few minutes. YouTube playback no longer fails on Android 7–12.
- **SponsorBlock choices.** Parents pick which kinds of marked parts to skip: sponsors, self-promotion and like-and-subscribe reminders by default, and optionally intros, outros, recaps and filler.
- **AI checks keep working** with AI models that reject the `temperature` setting, instead of holding every video for review.
- **Better search** and search-index sync to kids' devices.
- **Fire TV launcher icon** now shows instead of a placeholder.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
