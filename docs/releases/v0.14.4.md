# v0.14.4 - Podcast artwork, ready when you are

- **Podcast tiles show their cover straight away.** Home and search no longer flash a placeholder while the cover is looked up.
- **Podcast art loads ahead of time.** Covers and the art for each show's latest episodes download in the background, so opening a show feels as quick as opening a channel.
- **Newly added podcasts get their cover sooner.** A show just pushed from the parent's phone is fetched right away instead of waiting its turn.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
