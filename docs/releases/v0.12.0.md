# v0.12.0 - Custom playlists and kid reports

- **Add single videos.** Paste a video link (watch, youtu.be, shorts or live) into the channels box and put it in a **custom playlist** you create. A single video can only go into a custom playlist, never into a channel or YouTube playlist you've added. Each custom playlist is one item in your list with the usual screen-time chip and per-kid switches, and one row on the TV. Open it from the name to remove single videos, or remove the whole playlist with the bin.
- **Autoplay is your choice.** Under Playback, **Play the next video automatically** is off by default, so each video in a custom playlist ends back on the playlist. YouTube playlists continue as before.
- **Kids can report a video.** Hold OK on a tile and choose **Report this video**. It disappears at once and appears under **Waiting for your OK** on your phone, marked as reported by a kid, until you allow or block it. This works with AI screening off.
- **AI check switch.** Each channel (and each custom playlist) has a **Check with AI** switch in its 📝 dialog. Switch it off and its videos skip screening; the instructions box only shows while it's on.
- **Pairing picks the right address on Fire TV.** The QR code could carry an unreachable address on some Fire OS 7 devices, so the phone said it couldn't reach the TV. It now prefers the Wi-Fi or Ethernet address.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings. Update the TVs before using custom playlists: older builds don't understand them.
