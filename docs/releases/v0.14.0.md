# v0.14.0 - A new TV player

- **New player controls on the TV.** Press any arrow to see the title, chapter, time left and when the video ends, with big buttons for play/pause, previous/next, audio, subtitles and quality. Buttons only appear when they do something.
- **Scrub without losing your place.** ◀ ▶ move a marker along the timeline while the video keeps playing, and it jumps once when you stop or press OK. Hold to go faster, and press Back to cancel. A preview shows the time and chapter, plus a thumbnail when YouTube provides one.
- **Up next.** In a queue, a card shows what's coming in the last 10 seconds. OK plays it now and Back dismisses it.
- **Back steps out one level at a time,** from the panel to the controls to the video, before it leaves the player.
- **A cleaner focus look across the TV.** The white ring is gone. Tiles lift with a soft shadow and a white caption, buttons fill white, and rows get a soft highlight. Buttons are square on TV.
- **Blocked & reported page.** Parent settings has a new page listing what you and the AI blocked, with search. **Unblock** undoes a block you made.
- **Reports arrive sooner.** A video a kid reports on the TV now reaches the parent's phone even with AI screening off. It used to wait for the next verdict sync.
- **"Skipped ahead" hint.** A faint note appears in the top-right after a sponsor segment is skipped.
- **Clearer subtitle choices.** Auto-generated tracks now read "English (auto)".

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
