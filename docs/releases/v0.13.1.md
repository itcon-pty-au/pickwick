# v0.13.1 - Search for videos and playlists

- **Search YouTube for single videos and playlists, not just channels.** In **Content Sources → YouTube**, the search box now has three chips under it: **Channels** (the default), **Playlists** and **Videos**. Tap a chip to load that kind of result. Video results show a thumbnail, length and a red **LIVE** badge for streams on air, and Add puts the video into one of your custom playlists.
- **Back and Next.** Under the results, ‹ and › page through more results, and the screen scrolls back up to the search box each time. Pages you've already seen come back instantly.
- **A search icon instead of the word Search**, and the keyboard's Search key works too. A small ✕ in the box clears the text and the results.
- **Quicker repeat searches.** Results are remembered for ten minutes, so flipping between chips or going back to a query costs nothing.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
