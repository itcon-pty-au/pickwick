# v0.14.1 - Up next works from the remote

- **Reorder and remove with the remote.** On a TV, the ▲ ▼ ✕ buttons on Up next rows can now be reached with the arrow keys. They used to be skipped, so ▶ jumped to Play instead.
- **Clear several in a row.** After ✕ removes a video, focus moves to the ✕ of the next row, so each press of OK removes another video.
- **Playing from the list.** Tap or press OK on a video's thumbnail or title to play from there. On a phone, tapping the small gap around the buttons no longer starts playback.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
