# v0.10.0 - Podcasts

- Add podcasts by their RSS feed address under Content Sources > Podcasts. Pickwick checks the feed and fills in the show's name.
- Each podcast gets its own home tile, and opens onto an episode grid laid out like a YouTube channel. On TV, podcasts have their own row.
- Podcasts are audio-only and outside screen time: they don't use the daily budget, and breaks, blocked times, Pause everyone and content groups don't stop them. Give each podcast only to the children it suits.
- Episodes resume where they were left, play with the phone screen off, and have back 15 s / forward 30 s and previous/next episode controls.
- Hold an episode to add it to Up next, Favorites or Watch later, or to mark it watched. Up next can mix podcast episodes and videos in one queue, including with the screen off.
- Home and catalog tiles show a source icon: YouTube, network share or podcast.

Update every parent phone before adding podcasts: an older parent phone saves settings without them.

Install the signed `pickwick.apk` below to update without losing settings.
